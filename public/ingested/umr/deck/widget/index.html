Visitors

Four ways in.

A visitor opens the widget and picks one of four categories. The assistant asks the follow-up questions your team would ask, shows the case back for review, and only sends it once they confirm.

Custom Quote

Get a detailed quote for a new project. Walks the visitor through describing the job, with photos of the area and drawings with measurements.

Your team gets
Budget, materials, installed-on surface, photos

Existing Order

Track, update, or ask about an order. The visitor gives an order number and says what they need.

Your team gets
Order number and the change requested

Support

Installation help or a general question. Missing parts, damage or tension questions become a support ticket.

Your team gets
A ticket with the issue and photos

Talk to a Rep

Have someone reach out directly. The next rep in your rotation is assigned and gets the visitor's details.

Your team gets
A named owner, in turn, every time
Routing

One webhook, every tool.

Confirmed cases post to a single CRM webhook: a Zapier catch hook or a GoHighLevel inbound webhook. The payload is shaped for Zapier Paths, so one case can open a GHL opportunity, post a ready-made message to Slack and email the assigned rep.

Install

One line, then it's live.

The widget runs in a Shadow DOM, so your theme can't restyle it and it can't break your theme.

  1. Paste the script

    Add it right before </body> in your Shopify theme, or on any site.

  2. Point it at your CRM

    Set a Zapier catch hook or a GoHighLevel inbound webhook as the CRM webhook URL.

  3. Add your reps

    Put the people who take Talk to a Rep requests into the rotation, in the order you want.
