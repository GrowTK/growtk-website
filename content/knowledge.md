## About

Growtk is an AI-first outsourcing business for customer operations. It runs a
business's contact center, customer support, lead generation, sales support and
back office with AI agents and automation, and a person steps in on every
escalation, on the path the client chooses (their own team, a specific role or
an on-call rota). Growtk does not run a staffed call floor of human agents.

Growtk was founded in May 2026 by software engineers and is based in Lahore,
Pakistan. It works with clients remotely. Growtk builds the AI agents,
automations and integrations itself, inside the tools a client already uses.

## Services (13)

Contact center:
- Contact center services: inbound and outbound calls, chat, email and
  messaging handled by AI agents around the clock, with human escalation.
- Inbound contact center: every call, chat and email answered in seconds and
  resolved from the client's knowledge base and systems.
- Outbound contact center: reminders, renewals, callbacks and win-back calls
  and texts, only to contacts with the right consent, inside allowed hours.

Customer support:
- Customer support: support across every channel, tickets routed and tracked,
  people on hand for refunds, complaints and anything sensitive.
- Technical support: tier 1 troubleshooting from documentation, diagnostics
  collected up front, clean escalation to the client's engineers.
- E-commerce customer support: order status, returns, exchanges and pre-sale
  questions answered, connected to the store and carriers (often Shopify).
- Customer experience management: journey mapping, surveys after each
  touchpoint, review requests and replies, recovery for unhappy customers.

Sales and growth:
- Lead generation: every inquiry captured from forms, calls, chats and ads,
  qualified, routed to the right rep in seconds and followed up. Growtk does
  not buy or sell lead lists.
- Telemarketing: AI voice, text and email campaigns to the client's own
  consented contacts, with interested prospects handed to the sales team.
- Sales support: quote follow-up, meeting booking, CRM updates, pipeline stage
  workflows and team alerts (for example in Slack).

Operations:
- Back office support: data entry, document processing, invoicing,
  reconciliation, spreadsheets and alerts, with exceptions checked by a person.
- Workforce management: demand forecasting, scheduling, dispatch and AI agents
  absorbing peaks, for contact teams and field teams.
- Business process reengineering: process discovery, bottleneck analysis,
  redesign around AI and automation, then the build and before and after
  measurement.

## Solutions (4 systems Growtk builds for clients)

- Knowledge base system: a searchable help center and internal knowledge base
  written from real tickets, the same source the AI agents answer from.
- Helpdesk system: one queue for every call, chat, email and message, routed by
  topic and priority, with response targets and AI drafted replies.
- Quality management system: AI scores every call and chat against the
  client's scorecard, with a coaching queue and trends.
- Survey software: CSAT, NPS and post-contact surveys at the right moment, with
  low scores routed to a person the same day.

These are builds, set up inside the client's tools or built for them, not a
subscription product with a Growtk login.

## Process

1. Audit: a free call to map channels, volume and tools.
2. Design: a written plan of what AI agents handle, what goes to a person,
   what it connects to and what it costs.
3. Launch: built inside the client's tools, tested on real conversations,
   live one channel at a time.
4. Improve: weekly review of conversations, tuning, and more channels once the
   first runs clean.

A first channel is usually live in weeks, not months; the exact timeline is set
on the audit call.

## Security

Customer data stays in the client's own systems wherever possible, access is
limited by role, every automated action is logged, secrets are kept out of
code, and sensitive actions can require a person's approval. Growtk does not
hold security certifications and does not claim any.
